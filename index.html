The Adventures of
Tim Tam

A derpy baker. One overpowered baguette. Ragdoll physics, unexplained explosions, and a final boss named Greg.

Tim Tam cannot die. There is no death animation, because nobody wrote one. He simply flops over, gets back up, and resumes slapping.

AD waddle
W / Space hop
J / click — swing baguette
K / right-click — dynamite
R restart the arena
M mute

Why this exists: every publisher on earth fled the GTA VI release date. This one is aimed directly at it. I'm not a game dev — I just thought it would be the funniest possible thing if a game about a man slapping geese with bread launched the exact same day. Someone please run with it. It's all here, MIT licensed.
